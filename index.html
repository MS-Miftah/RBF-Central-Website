Who We Are

Driven by Compassion,
Backed by Nursing Excellence

Established in 13th January, 2025, Revive Blood Foundation (RBF) is a voluntary, non-political, and non-profit student-led healthcare organization based at the College of Nursing Sher-E-Bangla Nagar, Dhaka-1207 (CoNSBN).

Founded under the core motto "রক্ত দিন, মানবসেবায় অংশ নিন" (Donate Blood, Participate in Human Service), the foundation bridges the critical gap between hospital emergency beds and safe, immediate donors.

Managed entirely by the dedication of our campus ambassadors, student coordinators, and hundreds of passionate healthcare volunteers, RBF operates under the formal direct guidance of our respected institutional advisory panel.
